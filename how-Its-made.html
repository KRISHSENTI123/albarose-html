Christmas Delivery

To ensure your Alba Rose ashes jewellery arrives in time for Christmas, please place your order before Sunday, 1st December 2025.

Our jewellery is individually handcrafted with great care, and each piece takes several weeks to complete.

Orders placed after 1st December will continue to be accepted, but delivery before Christmas cannot be guaranteed

These orders will be scheduled for completion and dispatch during the second week of January 2026.

If you have already placed your order, please ensure that your ashes kit reaches us no later than Saturday, 7th December 2025.

Any ashes received after this date will mean your order will be completed after Christmas, even if it was placed before the ordering deadline.

We kindly encourage all customers wishing to gift a piece of memorial jewellery this Christmas to place their order early.

Ashes into Jewellery | How It’s Made

Discover how we transform ashes into jewellery with a Murano glass gemstone.

Handmade memorial jewellery crafted in-house by our skilled artisans, designed to last a lifetime.

/ How Its Made

Traditional Glassmaking

At the heart of our creations lies an age-old craft: lampworking. This traditional glassmaking technique uses a flame to melt glass rods at high temperatures, allowing our artisans to shape each gemstone by hand.

During the process, a carefully prepared token of ashes is delicately suspended within molten glass. As the glass cools and hardens, the ashes remain safely sealed inside, creating a one-of-a-kind gemstone that shimmers with colour and meaning.

Our glass gemstones are not only beautiful but also durable. The controlled process ensures each gemstone is strong enough to withstand everyday wear, while the natural variations of ashes into glass ensure that no two pieces are ever the same.

Authentic Venetian Murano Glass

To create our ashes gemstones, we use only the finest Venetian Murano glass, world-renowned for its purity, brilliance, and centuries-old tradition of craftsmanship. Murano glass has been treasured since the 14th centruy for its exceptional colour clarity and light-reflecting properties, making it the perfect medium for ashes jewellery. This traditional glassmaking technique uses a flame to melt glass rods at high temperatures, allowing our artisans to shape each gemstone by hand.

We offer a selection of 12 Murano glass colours, from soft pastels to radiant sky blues. Each hue has been chosen to enhance the natural sparkle of the glass and highlight the ashes within. Whether you prefer a delicate pale pink or a striking sapphire blue, there is a colour to reflect every personality and memory.

Hand-Crafted by Expert Goldsmiths

Once your Murano glass gemstone has been created, it is entrusted to our master goldsmiths. With years of expertise and traditional techniques, they carefully shape, set, and polish each piece by hand.

This stage is where artistry meets precision — ensuring your ashes jewellery is not only secure and comfortable to wear, but also finished with a beauty that reflects the memory it holds. Every curve, every setting, and every detail is designed to honour your loved one, creating a piece that is as meaningful as it is lasting.

Beautiful Gift Packaging With Every Purchase

Every piece of ashes jewellery from Alba Rose is presented in elegant, high-quality packaging, making it as special to receive as it is to wear. Your jewellery arrives in a luxury gift box, designed to protect your piece while offering a beautiful way to store it for years to come.

Whether you are buying for yourself or as a gift, our thoughtful memorial jewellery packaging adds an extra touch of care. Each presentation box reflects the quality and meaning of the jewellery inside — ensuring your tribute feels truly complete from the moment you open it.
