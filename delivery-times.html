Christmas Delivery

To ensure your Alba Rose ashes jewellery arrives in time for Christmas, please place your order before Sunday, 1st December 2025.

Our jewellery is individually handcrafted with great care, and each piece takes several weeks to complete.

Orders placed after 1st December will continue to be accepted, but delivery before Christmas cannot be guaranteed

These orders will be scheduled for completion and dispatch during the second week of January 2026.

If you have already placed your order, please ensure that your ashes kit reaches us no later than Saturday, 7th December 2025.

Any ashes received after this date will mean your order will be completed after Christmas, even if it was placed before the ordering deadline.

We kindly encourage all customers wishing to gift a piece of memorial jewellery this Christmas to place their order early.

Production & Delivery Times

We understand how important your jewellery is, and every piece is crafted with care and attention to detail. Below is a guide to how long the process usually takes.

Average Lead Times

On average, orders are completed within 2-3 weeks from the day we receive your ashes. Gold items typically take a little longer, around 4 weeks, as every gold item must be officially hallmarked at the Birmingham Assay Office — a legal requirement for all gold jewellery in the UK.

If you have a special date in mind, please let us know when you order. We’ll do our best to meet your request, wherever possible.

Order Steps

Throughout the process, we’ll keep you updated with email notifications at each key stage:

  • Step 1 - Ashes Received:

    As soon as your ashes arrive safely at our studio, we’ll send you an email confirmation. We then process the ashes ready for the glass gemstone to be made

  • Step 2 - Mount Being Made:

    Once the ashes have been processed, we then organise the precious metal mount to be made. Typical it takes 2–3 weeks to complete your order from this notification.

  • Step 3 - Glass Being Made:

    Whilst the mount is being made, we then organise the glass stone to be produced. Typically it takes 1–2 weeks to complete your order from this notification.

  • Step 4 - Hallmarking:

    If your order includes gold jewellery, it will be sent to the Birmingham Assay Office for hallmarking. This step usually takes an additional 3–5 days.

  • Step 5 - Dispatch & Delivery:

    Once your jewellery has been completed and passed our final quality checks, you’ll receive an email confirming dispatch. We ship all orders by Royal Mail Tracked 24. On the day of delivery, you’ll receive an email with a time window and the option to reschedule or collect from your local Post Office. For discretion, all parcels are sent in plain packaging.
