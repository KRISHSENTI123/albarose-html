Christmas Delivery

To ensure your Alba Rose ashes jewellery arrives in time for Christmas, please place your order before Sunday, 1st December 2025.

Our jewellery is individually handcrafted with great care, and each piece takes several weeks to complete.

Orders placed after 1st December will continue to be accepted, but delivery before Christmas cannot be guaranteed

These orders will be scheduled for completion and dispatch during the second week of January 2026.

If you have already placed your order, please ensure that your ashes kit reaches us no later than Saturday, 7th December 2025.

Any ashes received after this date will mean your order will be completed after Christmas, even if it was placed before the ordering deadline.

We kindly encourage all customers wishing to gift a piece of memorial jewellery this Christmas to place their order early.

Become an Authorised Retail Partner

By becoming an authorised retailer you can offer your clients the highest quality ashes jewellery in the UK generating additional
revenue for your business.

How Does it Work

  1. Once your account is approved, we'll send you a complimentary Point of Sale starter kit. This includes brochures, sample jewellery, and ashes collection kits to help you get started.
  2. You can place orders quickly and easily through our partner website, or by contacting our friendly team who will be happy to assist you.
  3. Our average turnaround time is 3 weeks from the moment we receive the ashes at our studio.

Become a Partner Today

We don't operate on a commission basis. Instead, you charge your customer the full Recommended Retail Price (RRP) as shown on our trade website. We will then invoice you for the RRP minus your agreed Trade Discount.

We understand the challenges of managing cash flow as a small business. That's why you won't need to pay until your order has been dispatched — or within 30 days of placing it, whichever comes first. This gives you time to collect payment from your customer before settling your invoice.

Quickly access your order history to view the products you've ordered for your customers — making new orders fast and hassle-free.

A variety of point-of-sale materials are available to order directly through your online trade account. Brochures, Freepost Envelopes & Ashes pouches to name a few.

With thousands of jewellery pieces crafted, your customers are in safe hands. If there's ever an issue with an order, our dedicated customer service team is here to help. Our in-house workshop offers ring resizing, polishing, repairs, and replating to ensure a swift turnaround.

By default, we ship orders directly to your business, allowing you to personally present the jewellery to your customer. Prefer it sent straight to the customer instead? Just let us know — we’re happy to arrange it.
