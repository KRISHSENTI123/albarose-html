Christmas Delivery

To ensure your Alba Rose ashes jewellery arrives in time for Christmas, please place your order before Sunday, 1st December 2025.

Our jewellery is individually handcrafted with great care, and each piece takes several weeks to complete.

Orders placed after 1st December will continue to be accepted, but delivery before Christmas cannot be guaranteed

These orders will be scheduled for completion and dispatch during the second week of January 2026.

If you have already placed your order, please ensure that your ashes kit reaches us no later than Saturday, 7th December 2025.

Any ashes received after this date will mean your order will be completed after Christmas, even if it was placed before the ordering deadline.

We kindly encourage all customers wishing to gift a piece of memorial jewellery this Christmas to place their order early.

Free Shipping

Every Alba Rose order comes with free shipping, no matter the value of your purchase.

We believe that something as meaningful as memorial jewellery should arrive with care and without extra cost. Your jewellery will be securely packaged and sent by tracked delivery, giving you peace of mind every step of the way.

Whether you are ordering for yourself or sending a heartfelt gift, delivery is always free — it’s our way of making the process as simple and stress-free as possible.

A complimentary Gift Box with every item.

Every piece of Alba Rose jewellery arrives in our luxurious gift packaging, designed to make the moment of receiving it as special as the jewellery itself.

Your chosen piece is carefully presented in a high-quality keepsake box, complete with our signature touches. Whether you are ordering for yourself or as a gift for someone dear, the presentation reflects the care, respect, and craftsmanship that goes into every item we create.

Certificate of Authenticity included.

Authenticity Certificate

With every piece of Alba Rose jewellery, you will receive a Certificate of Authenticity. This certificate is our promise that the gemstone we have created truly contains the ashes of your loved one, carefully and respectfully encased within the Murano glass.

We understand the deep trust you place in us when sending something so precious. The certificate provides you with reassurance and peace of mind — a written confirmation of the care, integrity, and craftsmanship behind your jewellery.

Each certificate is unique to your order and serves as a lasting record, ensuring you can treasure your jewellery with complete confidence.

3 Year Manufacturers Warranty.

3 Year Warranty

We want every piece of Alba Rose Ashes jewellery to be cherished for a lifetime, which is why we offer a comprehensive 3 Year Warranty on all our items.

Our warranty covers:

  • Manufacturing faults in materials or craftsmanship
  • Stone setting security, ensuring your glass gemstone is held safely.

What’s not covered:

  • Normal wear and tear (such as scratches or tarnishing).
  • Plating and finish , including rhodium or gold plating starting to wear away.
  • Damage caused by accidents, improper handling, or contact with chemicals.
  • Repairs or alterations made by another jeweller.

If you ever experience an issue within 3 years of purchase, simply contact our team and we will repair, replace, or restore your jewellery free of charge under the terms of our warranty.

We stand behind the quality of our craftsmanship and materials, giving you peace of mind that your jewellery is protected and made to last.

If your Alba Rose jewellery has some wear and tear, which is natural after years of wear, we offer a 10 day refurbishment service for just £59 per item to bring your jewellery back to its new condition.

Contact us to get your jewellery refurbished.

Free and easy returns process.

Return Policy

We understand how personal and meaningful each piece of ashes jewellery is. Because every item is custom made just for you, returns and refunds are limited — but we always want to help when something goes wrong.

When you can return an item?

We can accept a return only if:

  • The jewellery doesn’t match what you ordered (for example, wrong design, engraving, or glass colour).
  • There’s a manufacturing or quality fault.

If you believe one of these applies, please contact us within 14 days of receiving your order. Email us at support@albaroseashes.co.uk or call 01702 837012, including your order number, a description of the issue, and clear photographs if relevant

Once we review your case, we’ll arrange a repair, replacement, or return free of charge if it falls under our return policy.

Cancellations & refunds Because we personalise every piece using your entrusted ashes, we can only issue a full refund if:

  • You cancel before we begin work on your jewellery, or
  • We have not yet received your ashes.

Once production starts or your ashes arrive with us, we cannot offer a refund due to the nature of the product.
