Christmas Delivery

To ensure your Alba Rose ashes jewellery arrives in time for Christmas, please place your order before Sunday, 1st December 2025.

Our jewellery is individually handcrafted with great care, and each piece takes several weeks to complete.

Orders placed after 1st December will continue to be accepted, but delivery before Christmas cannot be guaranteed

These orders will be scheduled for completion and dispatch during the second week of January 2026.

If you have already placed your order, please ensure that your ashes kit reaches us no later than Saturday, 7th December 2025.

Any ashes received after this date will mean your order will be completed after Christmas, even if it was placed before the ordering deadline.

We kindly encourage all customers wishing to gift a piece of memorial jewellery this Christmas to place their order early.

Glass Guide

Our Glass Gemstones

Your Alba Rose jewellery begins with Venetian Murano glass, world-renowned for its purity, colour, and beauty.

Each glass gemstone is one of a kind, hand-made by our talented glass makers at our specialist production studio.

Into this beautiful glass, we enclose a small token of your loved one’s ashes, holding them inside a radiant gemstone that you can treasure forever.

The result is jewellery that holds both artistry and memory, a tribute you can wear close to your heart.

How to Order

We've made the process of ordering your Alba Rose ashes jewellery simple, secure, and filled with care at every stage.

Step 1: Choose Your Jewellery

Explore our collection of rings, pendants, bracelets, earrings and charms. Every piece can be personalised with your choice of Murano glass colour and precious metal, creating something truly special.

Step 2: Place Your Order

Once you've chosen your design, place your order securely through our website. If you prefer, you can place your order with your funeral director or crematorium.

Step 3: Receive Your Ashes Collection Kit

We'll send you a free collection kit with everything you need to securely send a small token of ashes to us.

Inside you'll find:

  • An order form with clear instructions
  • A secure foil ashes pouch and spoon
  • A prepaid tracked return envelope
  • A finger sizing tool

We only ever need a teaspoon of ashes to create your gemstone. Any that remain will be carefully returned to you with your finished jewellery.

Step 4: Your Gemstone is Crafted

If our glass studio, skilled artisans use traditional lampworking techniques with authentic Venetian Murano glass to encase the ashes within a gemstone. Each one is individually handmade, unique, and a lasting tribute to your loved one.

Step 5: Jewellery is Finished

Once your gemstone has been created, it is carefully set into your chosen jewellery mount by our expert jewellers. Every piece is polished, checked, and finished to the highest standard, ready to be treasured for years to come.

Step 6: Delivery of Your Jewellery

Your completed jewellery will be presented in our luxury gift packaging. We'll notify you by email as soon as it's on its way, ready for you to wear or to give as a meaningful gift.

3 Year Manufacturers Warranty.

3 Year Warranty

We want every piece of Alba Rose Ashes jewellery to be cherished for a lifetime, which is why we offer a comprehensive 3 Year Warranty on all our items.

Our warranty covers:

  • Manufacturing faults in materials or craftsmanship
  • Stone setting security, ensuring your glass gemstone is held safely.

What’s not covered:

  • Normal wear and tear (such as scratches or tarnishing).
  • Plating and finish , including rhodium or gold plating starting to wear away.
  • Damage caused by accidents, improper handling, or contact with chemicals.
  • Repairs or alterations made by another jeweller.

If you ever experience an issue within 3 years of purchase, simply contact our team and we will repair, replace, or restore your jewellery free of charge under the terms of our warranty.

We stand behind the quality of our craftsmanship and materials, giving you peace of mind that your jewellery is protected and made to last.

If your Alba Rose jewellery has some wear and tear, which is natural after years of wear, we offer a 10 day refurbishment service for just £59 per item to bring your jewellery back to its new condition.

Free and easy returns process.

Return Policy

We understand how personal and meaningful each piece of ashes jewellery is. Because every item is custom made just for you, returns and refunds are limited — but we always want to help when something goes wrong.

When you can return an item?

We can accept a return only if:

  • The jewellery doesn’t match what you ordered (for example, wrong design, engraving, or glass colour).
  • There’s a manufacturing or quality fault.

If you believe one of these applies, please contact us within 14 days of receiving your order. Email us at support@albaroseashes.co.uk or call 01702 837012, including your order number, a description of the issue, and clear photographs if relevant

Once we review your case, we’ll arrange a repair, replacement, or return free of charge if it falls under our return policy.

Cancellations & refunds Because we personalise every piece using your entrusted ashes, we can only issue a full refund if:

  • You cancel before we begin work on your jewellery, or
  • We have not yet received your ashes.

Once production starts or your ashes arrive with us, we cannot offer a refund due to the nature of the product.

Frequently Asked Questions

Please see below for answers to frequently asked questions.

  • We only need one teaspoon of ashes to make as many as 10 items of ashes jewellery. Any unused ashes are carefully returned with your completed order.

  • Each order is treated with great dignity and respect. Your ashes are securely barcoded, tracked, and kept separate throughout production so they can never be mixed or misplaced.

  • Most silver pieces take around 2-3 weeks from when the ashes arrive, and gold items take 3–4 weeks to complete due to the extra step of assay office hallmarking. We keep you updated at every stage of the process.

  • Our Murano glass gemstones are waterproof, scratch resistant and durable for everyday wear. However, like any fine jewellery, care should be taken to reduce the risk of impacts and damage.
