Christmas Delivery

To ensure your Alba Rose ashes jewellery arrives in time for Christmas, please place your order before Sunday, 1st December 2025.

Our jewellery is individually handcrafted with great care, and each piece takes several weeks to complete.

Orders placed after 1st December will continue to be accepted, but delivery before Christmas cannot be guaranteed

These orders will be scheduled for completion and dispatch during the second week of January 2026.

If you have already placed your order, please ensure that your ashes kit reaches us no later than Saturday, 7th December 2025.

Any ashes received after this date will mean your order will be completed after Christmas, even if it was placed before the ordering deadline.

We kindly encourage all customers wishing to gift a piece of memorial jewellery this Christmas to place their order early.

Return & Refund Policy

At Alba Rose Ashes, we know how meaningful each piece of cremation jewellery is. Every item is custom-made with care, using the ashes you've entrusted to us. Because of this personalisation, we're only able to accept returns or offer refunds under specific circumstances.

Returns

As each piece is uniquely created for you, we can only accept returns if:

  • The jewellery does not match your order (e.g. incorrect design, engraving, or glass colour), or
  • The jewellery has a manufacturing fault or quality issue

If you believe your item meets one of these criteria, please contact us within 14 days of receiving your order

You can reach our customer care team at:

  • support@albaroseashes.co.uk
  • 01702 837012

Please include:

  • Your order number
  • A description of the issue
  • Clear photographs (if applicable)

Once we have assessed the issue, we will arrange for a repair, replacement, or return at no cost to you, where applicable.

Cancellations & Refunds

If you need to cancel your order, please contact us as soon as possible.

We can offer a full refund only if:

  • Your cancellation request is made before any work has begun on your jewellery, or
  • We have not yet received your ashes.

Due to the personalised nature of our products, once production has started or ashes have been received, we're unable to offer a refund—even if the order is later cancelled.
