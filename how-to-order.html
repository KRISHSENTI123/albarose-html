Christmas Delivery

To ensure your Alba Rose ashes jewellery arrives in time for Christmas, please place your order before Sunday, 1st December 2025.

Our jewellery is individually handcrafted with great care, and each piece takes several weeks to complete.

Orders placed after 1st December will continue to be accepted, but delivery before Christmas cannot be guaranteed

These orders will be scheduled for completion and dispatch during the second week of January 2026.

If you have already placed your order, please ensure that your ashes kit reaches us no later than Saturday, 7th December 2025.

Any ashes received after this date will mean your order will be completed after Christmas, even if it was placed before the ordering deadline.

We kindly encourage all customers wishing to gift a piece of memorial jewellery this Christmas to place their order early.

Ashes Into Murano Glass

At Alba Rose, we’ve made the process of creating your ashes jewellery simple, secure, and respectful.
Each step is designed to give you peace of mind, from sending your order kit to receiving your finished ashes jewellery.

Step 1: Choose Your Ashes Jewellery

Browse our collection of ashes to glass jewellery, including rings, pendants, bracelets and earrings. Each design can be personalised with your choice of Murano glass colour and precious metal.

Step 2: Place Your Order Online

Once you’ve chosen your design, simply place your order through our secure website checkout. You’ll then receive a free order kit with everything you need to safely return a small token of ashes to us using a prepaid tracked return envelope.

Alternatively, we partner with hundred of funeral directors and crematoriums who can handle the order on your behalf. Simply speak to your existing funeral director for assistance in placing an order

Step 3: Return Your Ashes Collection Kit

Your order kit includes:

  • Full instructions on how to ship the ashes.
  • A secure foil pouch and spoon for the ashes
  • Pre-paid tracked return envelope

We only need a small level teaspoon of ashes to create your gemstone. Any remaining ashes are carefully returned with your finished jewellery.

Step 4: Your Glass Gemstone is Made

In our UK studio, our specialists use traditional glassmaking techniques to fuse a small amount of ashes into hand-blown Murano glass, creating a true tribute — a beautiful finish that will last a lifetime.

Step 5: Your Jewellery is Finished

Once your gemstone has been created, it is carefully set into your chosen jewellery mount by our expert jewellers. Every piece is polished, checked and — if gold — sent for hallmarking. You'll receive photo updates as we work.

Step 6: Delivery of Your Order

Once complete, your jewellery is presented in our luxury gift packaging, ready to treasure or give as a thoughtful gift. We’ll notify you by email when your piece is on its way.
